## 🚀 Elevate Your Guest Experience
In today’s digital-first world, guests expect seamless, contactless, and instant service. BlueOnyx Hotel CMS isn't just a management tool; it's a **digital transformation** for your property.

### ✨ Key Features at a Glance

#### 📱 Next-Gen QR Room Service
*   **Total Autonomy:** Every room gets a unique, beautiful QR code. 
*   **Scan-to-Order:** Guests browse your premium digital menu, select multiple items, and order directly from their own devices—no app download required.
*   **Zero Friction:** Guests provide their name and mobile number, and the system automatically identifies their room number.

#### 🏢 Multi-Hotel Management
*   **Centralized Control:** Manage an entire portfolio of hotels from a single Super Admin dashboard.
*   **Real-time Analytics:** Monitor occupancy, revenue, and service efficiency across all properties in one view.

#### 🛏️ Intelligent Inventory & Booking
*   **Visual Management:** Real-time room status tracking (Available, Occupied, Maintenance).
*   **Smart Room Types:** Categorize your inventory with custom pricing, amenities, and capacity settings.
*   **Comprehensive Booking Manager:** Handle check-ins, check-outs, and guest profiles with a high-performance console.

#### 🔔 Live Service Operations
*   **Kitchen & Staff Console:** A dedicated, live "Service Requests" console for staff to manage orders as they arrive.
*   **Status Tracking:** Move orders through *Pending*, *Accepted*, *In-Progress*, and *Completed* stages with a click.
*   **Pulse Analytics:** An executive dashboard showing you exactly how your service is performing right now.

## 🛠️ The BlueOnyx Tech Stack
We built BlueOnyx using the **"Gold Standard"** of modern web technologies to ensure 99.9% uptime, enterprise-grade security, and lightning-fast performance.

*   **Frontend:** `React.js` + `Tailwind CSS` + `Vite` for a silky-smooth, premium user interface.
*   **Backend:** `Node.js` + `Express` for a robust, scalable API architecture.
*   **Database:** `PostgreSQL` for rock-solid data integrity and complex relational handling.
*   **Aesthetics:** `Lucide React` icons and a curated design system that "Wows" at first glance.
